### New

- The Highlighted Books tab has a search bar, so you can find a book without scrolling through the whole list.
- New "Turn All Off" button in the Highlighted Books tab, to switch every book off at once. Nothing changes until you save.
- New "Bookmarks in This World" button to clear every bookmark in the world or server you are playing on. It asks before clearing, and other worlds and servers keep their bookmarks.

### Changes

- "Price Offset" is replaced by "Good Deal Range". Instead of a fixed number of emeralds, it counts the cheapest share of a book's possible prices as a good deal, so low and high level books are judged the same way. The default is 5%. If you had changed Price Offset, set Good Deal Range again.
