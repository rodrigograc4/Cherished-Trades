**New**
- Look at a villager to see its bookmarked trades above its head, under its name if it has one.
- Hover over an enchanted book to see every price it can have, what this villager asks, and the lasting discount you get from curing it.
- New "Enable Highlighted Books" setting to turn highlighting off without losing your list.

**Changes**
- Highlighted books are now picked with Yes/No toggles in their own settings tab, instead of typing names.
- Mending and Unbreaking III are highlighted by default.
- The price checker now looks at the villager's own price, so temporary discounts like Hero of the Village no longer make a bad deal look good.

**Fixes**
- The text box for adding highlighted books was invisible.
