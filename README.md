# 📜 Cherished Trades

**Cherished Trades** makes villager trading easier. Pin the trades you care about, spot the enchanted books you are hunting for, and see at a glance whether a price is a good deal.

It is a lightweight, **client-side** mod: it works in singleplayer and on any server, with nothing to install on the server.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔖 Bookmark trades

Click the bookmark next to a trade to pin it to the top of the list. Bookmarks are remembered per villager and per world or server, so your favourite trades are always first when you come back.

![Bookmarked trades pinned to the top of the trade list](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rodrigograc4/Cherished-Trades/main/assets/bookmarks.png)
<br><br>

### 🏷️ See bookmarks above villagers

Look at a villager and its bookmarked trades appear above its head, under its name if it has one. No more opening every villager in your trading hall to find the Mending librarian.

![A named villager with its bookmarked trades under its name](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rodrigograc4/Cherished-Trades/main/assets/villager-labels.png)
<br><br>

### 📚 Highlighted books

Choose the enchanted books you are looking for, and any trade selling one is highlighted. Great for rerolling librarians. Mending and Unbreaking III are selected by default.

![A Mending trade highlighted in purple](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rodrigograc4/Cherished-Trades/main/assets/highlighted-books.png)
<br><br>

### 💰 Price checker

A gold arrow marks enchanted books sold at the lowest price a villager can roll. It looks at the villager's own price, so temporary discounts such as Hero of the Village don't make a bad deal look good.

![Gold arrows on the cheapest enchanted book trades](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rodrigograc4/Cherished-Trades/main/assets/price-checker.png)
<br><br>

Hover over a book to see every price it can have, what this villager asks, and the lasting discount you get from curing it.

![Trade screen with bookmarks, a highlighted book, gold arrows and the price tooltip](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rodrigograc4/Cherished-Trades/main/assets/price-tooltip.png)
<br>

## ⚙️ Configuration

Open the settings from [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu).

- **Enable Bookmarks**: show the bookmark icons next to trades.
- **Enable Bookmark Labels**: show the bookmarked trades above the villager you are looking at. Turn it off to keep using bookmarks without the labels.
- **Enable Highlighted Books**: highlight the books picked in the _Highlighted Books_ tab. Turning it off keeps your choices.
- **Enable Price Checker**: show the gold arrow and the price tooltip.
- **Price Offset**: also count prices slightly above the lowest as a good deal. An offset of 2 means "lowest price + 2".

![General settings](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rodrigograc4/Cherished-Trades/main/assets/settings.png)
<br><br>

In the **Highlighted Books** tab, every enchantment villagers can sell is listed, one toggle per level.

![Highlighted Books settings](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rodrigograc4/Cherished-Trades/main/assets/settings-books.png)
<br>

## 📦 Requirements

- Minecraft **26.3**
- [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/) and [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)
- [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) _(optional, to open the settings)_

Cloth Config is already included.
